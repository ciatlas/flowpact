## What you see

In the [`call-permissions`](https://github.com/ciatlas/flowpact/tree/main/fixtures/call-permissions) fixture:

```text
✖  ERROR  FP613 ungranted-permissions
  .github/workflows/publish.yml › jobs.npm requests contents: write, packages: write, but jobs.publish grants contents: read, packages: none
  ▶ .github/workflows/release.yml:5:1
```

## Why it matters

A called workflow's jobs run with the caller's `GITHUB_TOKEN` and can only keep or lower its permissions. GitHub refuses the whole run when it starts ("The nested job … is requesting 'contents: write', but is only allowed 'contents: read'").

## How to fix

Grant the permission in the calling job's `permissions:`; a job's `permissions:` replaces the workflow's for that job only. If the called job does not need it, lower its `permissions:` instead.

## Example

<Tabs items={["Problem", "Fixed"]}>
<Tab value="Problem">

```yaml
permissions:
  contents: read
jobs:
  release:
    uses: ./.github/workflows/release.yml   # its job publish requests contents: write
```

</Tab>
<Tab value="Fixed">

```yaml
jobs:
  release:
    uses: ./.github/workflows/release.yml
    permissions:
      contents: write
```

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Scope

What a call grants is the calling job's `permissions:`, else its workflow's. With neither, it gets the repository's or organization's default, which flowpact cannot see, so the call is not checked; when the calling workflow is itself called, the workflows it calls are checked against what its callers grant instead. What a called job requests is its `permissions:`, else its workflow's (reported once for every job that uses them); a job with neither gets what the call grants. A called job that calls another workflow requests its permissions for all of it, and the jobs of that workflow are checked against them at that call. Jobs are checked whatever their `if:`, as GitHub checks them before any job starts. A remote reusable workflow is not checked: the remote interfaces lockfile does not record what its jobs request. Whether a job requests more than it needs is not checked.

## Accept one finding

When a finding is intended, accept it with a reason rather than turning the rule off. It is listed apart from the other
findings, and does not fail the build.

**On its line**, with an [ignore comment](/docs/configuration#ignore-comments) alone on the line above it (the
editor's quick fix adds it there) or at the end of the line, after any other comment such as a version

**In the config**, with an [override](/docs/configuration#overrides), which can expire:

```yaml title=".github/flowpact/flowpact.config.yml"

  - rule: FP613
    target: ".github/workflows/release.yml#jobs.publish.permissions"
    reason: Why it is acceptable here, at least 10 characters.
    expires: YYYY-MM-DD   # optional
```

flowpact lint --format json | jq -r '.findings[] | select(.code == "FP613") | .symbol'
