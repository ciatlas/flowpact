## What you see

In the [`ignore-comments`](https://github.com/ciatlas/flowpact/tree/main/fixtures/ignore-comments) fixture:

```text
▲  WARN  FP905 ignore-comment-unused
  Ignore comment for FP108 matches no finding on line 22
  ▶ .github/workflows/release.yml:22:74
```

## Why it matters

The problem was fixed, or the comment is on another line than the finding. Either way it would hide a later finding of that rule there that nobody looked at.

## How to fix

Delete the rule from the comment, or the comment when it names no other. A comment accepts the findings reported on its line; alone on its line, those on the next line of YAML; on a block scalar’s line (`run: |`), those in its text.
